First release.

Zones and DNS with a real form for each of the 21 record types, cache purge,
zone settings, analytics, WAF custom rules and rate limiting, IP access rules,
Workers, Pages, KV, D1, R2, and Zero Trust tunnels, Access apps and Gateway
rules. Anything without a dedicated screen is reachable through an explorer
that builds a form from the endpoint's own schema — all 3240 of them.

Credentials are encrypted on the device under a PIN-derived key; biometric
unlock is enforced by the Keystore rather than the UI. No telemetry.

Eight languages, light and dark themes, dynamic colour.
